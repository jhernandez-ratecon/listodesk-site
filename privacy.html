Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

ListoDesk ("we") runs listodesk.com and RateCon Pack. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Early-access emails: if you email us, we keep your email address and message so we can reply and tell you when the product opens.
  • Product data (once live): documents you upload, such as rate confirmations and PODs, plus the load details pulled from them, used only to provide the service.
  • Missed-call data (once live): caller phone numbers, call times and the texts callers send, used only to reply with your message and build your summaries. Callers can reply STOP to opt out.

This website uses no analytics or advertising trackers and sets no cookies.

How we use it

We use it to provide and support the service and to contact you about your account or early access. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers needed to run the product, such as hosting and email, when the law requires it, or when you tell us to (for example, sending a packet to your broker).

Your choices

To see, change or delete your information, email hello@listodesk.com.

Changes

If we update this policy, we'll post the new version here.
