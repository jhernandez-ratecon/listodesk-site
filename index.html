How it works

Rate con paperwork

1

Send the rate con

Forward it to your private RateCon Pack address or upload it from your phone.
2

Check the details

Load #, broker, stops, dates, rate and accessorials are pulled out for you. Fix anything with a tap.
3

Add the POD

Snap a photo of the signed BOL/POD.
4

Send the packet

Get one PDF (invoice, rate con and POD) and send it to your broker or factor.

Missed-call replies

1

Set your message

For example: "On the road/resting. Text me the load details or I'll call back at 6 PM."
2

Miss a call

Unanswered calls are forwarded to your ListoDesk line, so you don't have to touch your phone.
3

Caller gets your text

ListoDesk texts the caller your message and logs any load details they send back.
4

Read a quick summary

When you stop, see who called, from which company, and the lane, rate and dates they sent.

FAQ

Is ListoDesk available now?We're opening early access. Join the list and we'll email you when your spot is ready.
Does it work with any broker's rate con?It's built for standard broker rate confirmation PDFs. You can always review and correct the fields before the invoice is made.
Does it work with my factoring company?The packet is a standard PDF (invoice, rate con and POD) that you send wherever you normally submit. We don't connect to your factor's account.
Do I need a new phone number?No. You keep your number. Your phone forwards calls you don't answer to your ListoDesk line, which sends your reply text and logs the details.
Does it work while I'm driving?Yes, hands-free. Replies go out automatically, so you don't need to touch your phone until you stop.
Is it compliant? Will it spam people?It only texts people who just called you, and only with the message you write. It never cold-texts or sends marketing. Anyone can reply STOP to stop getting texts.
Do you touch my money or bank account?No. RateCon Pack builds paperwork only. Payments go from your broker or factor directly to you.
Can I cancel?Yes, anytime. No contracts.
¿Está en español?Sí. La aplicación funciona en inglés y en español.
